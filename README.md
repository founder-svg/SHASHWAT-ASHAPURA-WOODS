# Shashwat Ashapura Woods — Website Project

A premium, modern, and nature-inspired web application for **Shashwat Ashapura Woods**, a premier direct importer, stockist, and bulk supplier of **Shutter Plywood**, **Shutter Pinewood**, and certified global **Pinewood Species**.

---

## 🌲 Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Targets

- **Visual Concept:** *Nature → Timber → Processing → Plywood → Construction*
- **Brand Palette:**
  - **Deep Forest Green** (`#123C2A`) — Header, hero overlays, product highlights, footer
  - **Wood Brown** (`#7A4E2D`) — Accents, icons, secondary buttons, warm timber feel
  - **Natural Cream** (`#F5F0E7`) — Content backgrounds, editorial sections, cards
  - **Charcoal** (`#1D211E`) — High-contrast typography & headings
  - **Amber Gold** (`#C69255`) — Subtle highlights, badges, and active indicators
- **Typography:**
  - Headings: *Cormorant Garamond* & *Cinzel* (Sophisticated editorial serif & display)
  - Body & Navigation: *Plus Jakarta Sans* (Clean, legible, modern geometric sans-serif)
